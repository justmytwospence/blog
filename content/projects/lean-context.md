lean-context is retired. For Pi, use [cache-guard](/projects/cache-guard), which combines tool-output trimming and compaction with cache-cost visibility in [Pi Plugins](/projects/pi-plugins). Do not enable the retired Pi extension alongside its replacement.

The opencode port went the same way: its trimming and compaction are part of opencode-cache-guard in [OpenCode Plugins](/projects/opencode-plugins).

This page preserves the original Pi and opencode repositories for reference. Neither is a component of either suite.
